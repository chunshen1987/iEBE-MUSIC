# Systematic Measurements of Identified Particle Spectra in pp, d+Au and Au+Au Collisions from STAR
[paper](https://inspirehep.net/literature/793126)

[data](https://inspirehep.net/literature/793126)

## Kinematic cuts
1. Full azimuthal angle $\phi$ from 0 to $2\pi$ coverage.
2. $|\eta|$ < 0.1
3. Minimum $p_T$ 0.2 GeV.


## Notes
1. The data for identified particle dN/dy and mean pT is in the paper itself. To get dN/dy, extrapolation using hydrodynamics inspired Blast Wave fit was used for higher pT.
