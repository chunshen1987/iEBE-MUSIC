# Anisotropic flow of charged particles in Pb-Pb collisions at 5.02 TeV

[paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1602.01119)
[hepdata](https://www.hepdata.net/record/ins1419244)

## Kinematic cuts
Centrality: V0A ($\eta \in [2.8, 5.1]$) + V0C ($\eta \in [-3.7, -1.7]$)
Particle of interest: $|\eta| < 0.8$, $p_T \in [0.2, 5]$ GeV
                      with an gap $|\Delta \eta| > 1$ for two-particle correlations

## Notes
- In theoretical analysis, we will use two subevents for two-particle correlations:
  one with $\eta \in [0.5, 0.8]$ and one with $\eta \in [-0.8, -0.5]$.
- For 4-particle correlations, we compute it with Qn vectors from
  2-subevents and 1-event.
- We use all charged hadrons with $p_T \in [0.2, 4]$ GeV as the contributions
  from $p_T \in [4, 5]$ GeV from hydrodynamic simualtions are negligible.
